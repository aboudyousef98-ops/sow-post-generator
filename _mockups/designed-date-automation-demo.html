The three rules

1Fill only when blank Saving a draft stamps the linked project's Designed date — only if it's empty. 35% of your date-sets are "tomorrow" planning; those are never touched.
2Local day, not UTC The stamp uses the coordinator's local date. 40% of evening saves would otherwise land on yesterday.
3Manual always wins The column stays editable exactly as today. The stamp is a default, logged in the activity feed like any other change.
