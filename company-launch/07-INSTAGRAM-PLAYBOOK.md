# 07 — Instagram Playbook

## Setup tasks
1. Create account with the business email; switch to Professional → Business; category "Solar Energy Service" or "Design".
2. Create a Facebook Page and link it (needed for Meta Business Suite scheduling).
3. Bio: "Permit-ready solar plan sets for US installers · PE-stamped via licensed partners · CA TX FL AZ" + site link.
4. Profile image: logo mark on navy. Highlights: Services, Samples, Code Notes, Partners.
5. Scheduling: Meta Business Suite (free). Verify how far ahead it allows scheduling at setup time; plan to load the calendar in batches.

## Grid system
Navy background, white linework, amber accent, title-block corner with series tag and sheet number. Alternate: carousel, reel, single image.

## Calendar reality check
- 2 posts/week × 52 weeks = 104 slots. 50 posts cover 25 weeks.
- Options: (a) 1 new + 1 repurposed per week (reel cut of a carousel, quote card of an article) = full year from 50 masters; (b) generate 104 in the sprint; (c) schedule 6 months, refresh mid-year.
- Recommendation: (a). Evergreen code content repurposes well, and a mid-year check keeps code-cycle references current.

## Rules
- Label concept work in the image and caption.
- Use Instagram's AI label for AI-generated video/images where applicable.
- Captions: hook line, 3–5 lines, CTA, 5–8 hashtags.
- Alt text on every post.
- Leave 20% of slots flexible for real news (first partner, first client, site launch).
