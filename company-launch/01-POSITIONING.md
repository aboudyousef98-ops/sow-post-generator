# 01 — Positioning

## One-liner
[NAME] produces permit-ready residential solar plan sets for US installers, PE-stamped through licensed partners.

## Customer
- **Primary:** small and mid-size residential solar installers/EPCs (roughly 5–100 installs per month) without an in-house design team, or with overflow.
- **Secondary:** solar sales organizations, roofing companies adding solar, electricians doing battery retrofits.
- **Not the customer:** homeowners. All copy is B2B.

## Offer (launch)
| Deliverable | Contents |
|---|---|
| PV permit plan set | Cover sheet, site plan, roof/array layout, attachment detail, single-line or three-line diagram, electrical calcs, placards/labels, equipment data sheets |
| Structural letter | Roof framing check for added PV dead load, wind and snow per ASCE 7 as adopted locally, stamped by partner PE |
| Electrical stamp | Where the AHJ requires it, by partner PE |
| Revisions | AHJ correction responses |
| Add-ons | Battery/ESS, main panel upgrade, utility interconnection application package |

## Differentiators to prove, not just claim
1. AHJ-specific accuracy (jurisdiction requirement notes on every set).
2. Predictable turnaround (state a number only after it is measured on concept sets).
3. One point of contact who is a designer, not a ticket queue.
4. Clean, readable sheets (the blueprint brand is the product).

## Competitive frame
Established plan-set vendors compete on price and speed at volume. A new entrant wins on quality, responsiveness and niche depth (e.g. battery retrofits, difficult AHJs in the four launch states), not on being cheapest.

## First markets
CA, TX, FL, AZ. Content and partner PE search start there. Website lists only states with a signed PE partner as "stamping available"; the rest as "design only, stamping coming".

## Pricing
Placeholder until competitor study: per-project flat fee with tiers (PV only / PV + battery / with structural letter / with both stamps). Decide in phase 4.

## Messages
- "Permit-ready the first time."
- "Designed for your AHJ, not a generic template."
- "Drafted by solar designers. Stamped by licensed PEs."

## Risks
| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Looks like an empty shell | Real sample sets, real educational depth, named founder |
| No PE partner at launch | PE partnership posts in month 1; site wording stays truthful |
| Too broad | Stay on residential PV permits until first 10 clients |
