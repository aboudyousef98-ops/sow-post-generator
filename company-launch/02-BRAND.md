### Checks (Chrome side panel task)
1. `.com` available, or acceptable `.design` / `.solar` / `get[name].com`.
2. Instagram and LinkedIn handle available, same spelling.
3. USPTO trademark search (classes 42 and 37) shows no live conflicting mark.
4. Google the name + "solar": no existing company.
5. Secretary of State business name search in the LLC state.
6. Say it on a phone call once; spell it once.

## Visual identity: blueprint technical
| Token | Value | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Navy | `#0B1F3A` | Backgrounds, headings |
| Blueprint blue | `#1E4E8C` | Secondary surfaces, links |
| Paper | `#F5F7FA` | Light backgrounds |
| Line grey | `#8A9BB0` | Grid, dimension lines |
| Solar amber | `#F5A623` | Single accent: CTAs, highlights |
| Ink | `#101820` | Body text on light |

Check amber-on-navy and text contrast against WCAG AA before finalizing.

- **Type:** Space Grotesk (headings), Inter (body), JetBrains Mono (annotations, sheet labels). All free.
- **Motifs:** drafting grid, dimension lines, leader arrows, title-block corner on post templates, sheet numbers ("A-101") as post series tags.
- **Photography/AI visuals:** roofs, arrays and line drawings over navy; no fake crews or branded trucks.

## Logo brief
- Wordmark plus a simple mark that works at 32 px (favicon, profile circle).
- Mark ideas: tilted module as a parallelogram with a dimension line; sun arc drawn as a compass sweep; monogram inside a title-block corner.
- Deliver: SVG, PNG on navy and on white, single-colour version, profile-circle crop.
- Route: 3 concepts in Canva or Figma, pick one, vectorize.

## Voice
Precise, calm, helpful. Short sentences. Cite the code section when stating a rule. No hype words, no exclamation marks, no promises about savings.

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| "NEC 690.12 requires rapid shutdown on this roof." | "We're the #1 solar designers." |
| "Sample project, concept design." | "Another happy client." (when there is none) |
