# 06 — LinkedIn Playbook

Executed with Claude in the Chrome side panel; you log in, handle 2FA and approve each publish.

## Setup tasks (in order)
1. Founder profile first: headline, about, featured link. A company page needs a real personal profile behind it.
2. Create Company Page: name, tagline "Solar Permit Design", industry, size "2–10" only if true (otherwise "1"), website, logo (300×300), cover (1128×191).
3. About section from `01-POSITIONING.md`; specialties: PV plan sets, permit design, structural letters, NEC 690/705, ESS.
4. Add custom button: "Visit website" → quote page.
5. Founder lists the company as current position.
6. Invite connections (LinkedIn limits monthly invites).

## Content mix (LinkedIn is the B2B channel)
| Type | Share | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Educational | 40% | "3 reasons Florida AHJs reject plan sets" |
| Concept project breakdown | 20% | Carousel of a labeled sample set |
| Process / behind the drawing | 15% | How we check roof framing before layout |
| PE partnership | 10% | "Licensed PE in TX or AZ? We're building our review network." |
| Talent network | 5% | "Solar designers: join our roster for future project work." |
| Company news | 10% | Site launch, new state coverage |

Cadence: 2 posts/week on the page; founder reshares with a personal comment (personal posts get more reach than page posts).

## Partnership and hiring post rules
- PE posts are real today: state the states, the scope (review and stamp of residential PV), and how to reply.
- Talent-network posts say "roster" and "project-based, as work comes in".
- Use LinkedIn's Jobs feature only for an actual open role.

## Side-panel guardrails
- One action at a time, human-speed; no bulk connection or messaging automation.
- Claude drafts and fills; you click Post/Schedule.
- Use LinkedIn's native scheduler (up to about 3 months ahead); re-schedule quarterly.

## Per-post brief format
`ID | date | type | hook | body | asset file | alt text | label (Concept/AI) | CTA | hashtags (3–5)`
